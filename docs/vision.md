# 🌟 Pathloom — Vision Document

> The guiding philosophy and product vision for Pathloom.

---

## Mission

Help people discover careers, identify skill gaps, plan their education, and navigate global opportunities — all through AI-powered intelligence in a single platform.

---

## The Problem

Today, students and professionals must visit **8+ fragmented platforms** to answer fundamental career and life questions:

| Question | Where They Go Today |
|----------|-------------------|
| "Which career should I choose?" | LinkedIn, Career websites |
| "What skills should I learn?" | Roadmap.sh, YouTube |
| "How much salary will I earn?" | Levels.fyi, Glassdoor |
| "Which university should I apply to?" | QS Rankings, university websites |
| "Can I get a scholarship?" | Scholarship portals |
| "What visa route should I take?" | Government visa websites |
| "How do I reach my dream career?" | ChatGPT, YouTube, Reddit |
| "Which country should I move to?" | Multiple research sources |

**The result:** Information overload, decision paralysis, and suboptimal life choices.

---

## The Solution

**Pathloom** unifies all of this into a single AI-powered platform with three planning pillars:

### 1. Career Planning
Understand your career readiness, discover fitting roles, identify skill gaps, and get a prioritized learning roadmap.

### 2. Study Planning
Find the right universities, predict admission chances, match scholarships, and plan your academic journey.

### 3. Global Mobility Planning
Discover the best countries for your career, understand visa pathways, compare salaries and cost of living worldwide.

---

## Core Philosophy

Most career platforms answer: *"What can I do today?"*

**Pathloom answers:** *"What should I do over the next 5 years?"*

The platform doesn't just assess the present — it plans a **multi-year trajectory** combining career progression, education, and global mobility into one unified path.

---

## Product Modules

### Module 1: 💼 Career Intelligence
- Career analysis with readiness scoring
- Role recommendations based on existing skills
- Side-by-side career comparison (salary, demand, difficulty)
- Multi-country salary intelligence
- Market trend analysis
- Resume analysis and AI feedback
- AI Career Coach (conversational)
- 5-Year Career Planner

### Module 2: 🎓 Study Intelligence
- University finder with ranking and program data
- Admission prediction with tier classification
- Scholarship matching with eligibility analysis
- Study cost calculator
- Study roadmap generation
- Student visa planning
- AI Study Coach (conversational)

### Module 3: 🌎 Global Opportunity Intelligence
- Country recommendation engine
- Immigration and visa pathway intelligence
- Migration planning with step-by-step guidance
- Global salary ranking
- Opportunity scoring per country

### Module 4: 🤖 AI Future Planner *(Flagship)*
The user enters their current profile (age, country, degree, career goal) and Pathloom generates a complete life plan:
- Recommended career path
- Target country
- Expected salary
- Required skills and learning plan
- Language requirements
- University recommendations
- Scholarship matches
- Visa route
- Timeline with milestones
- Success probability
